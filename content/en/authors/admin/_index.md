I am an **AI Infrastructure Researcher** in the Computational Mathematics Group, Theory Research Department, 2012 Laboratories at Huawei Technologies. My work spans **LLM training, inference and data infrastructure**.

I independently develop **[APIE](https://github.com/CAKE-math/APIE)**, an LLM inference framework designed for recursive self-improvement (RSI), with optimization skills, benchmarks and validation tools for coding agents. I have also worked on openPangu MTP architecture selection and full-graph inference, DeepSeek performance optimization on Ascend, and distributed pretraining data pipelines. My engineering interests cover GPU/NPU systems, serving, communication overlap and data processing.

I received my master's degree from Georgia Tech and studied at Zhejiang University's ZJU-UIUC Institute. My earlier research interests include computer graphics and 3D scene reconstruction.
